# TaxCal
Federal tax estimate only. This calculator estimates ordinary federal income tax and Alternative Minimum Tax based on the assumptions entered (standard deduction, single or married filing jointly, and [2026 tax brackets](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-releases-tax-inflation-adjustments-for-tax-year-2026-including-amendments-from-the-one-big-beautiful-bill)). It does not calculate payroll taxes, capital gains, tax credits, AMT credit carryforwards, state taxes, withholding, or all IRS adjustments. ISO qualification and AMT treatment may require additional analysis. Use at your own risk.
